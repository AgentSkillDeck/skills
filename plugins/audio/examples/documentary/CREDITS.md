## About this example

Original script (agentskilldeck). A 52-second documentary in three chaptered scenes, voiced by the optional Qwen3-TTS engine on Apple Silicon: each line's emotion goes to the model as an instruction, and each speaker has a `style:` persona.

- Rendered by `produce.mjs` on 2026-11-17 with Qwen3-TTS 1.7B CustomVoice (8-bit MLX, mlx-audio 0.6.6); QA clean on the first take (6 segments, whisper small).
- `mix.mp3` is produce.mjs's own file (under 1 MB). Mastered to −14.1 LUFS, true peak −1.3 dBTP.
- The wind is generated by ffmpeg, so there is nothing to credit or fetch: re-rendering needs no network.
